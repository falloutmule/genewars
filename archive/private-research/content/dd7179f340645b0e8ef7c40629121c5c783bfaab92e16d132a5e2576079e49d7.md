# GeneWars development policy - repo copy (adopted 2026-10-17)

Operative policy for future GeneWars director/worker assignments. The canonical text with the
full detail lives in the active Hermes skill `genewars-reconstruction`
(`references/family-assignment-policy.md`, `references/worker-task-packet-template.md`); this
repo copy exists so a worker packet can cite a path inside the checkout.

## Family-based assignment

- One assignment = one **bounded, source-backed family of related transitions** with its
  dependencies, not the next frame and not one narrow callback.
- Every packet names: the family and its declared caller/transition domains; **all affected
  callers (owned AND neutral)**; the shared prefix/phase/animation/motion/cell-commit ordering;
  which semantics genuinely differ (owner, target, task, association, reference, RNG); the
  source-proved accepted cases; the explicit unsupported/unknown cases; the focused fixtures and
  integration inputs; the evidence needed to close the family; and a **caller/domain matrix**
  updated whenever a shared helper changes.
- Statuses: `integrated/proved`, `focused-only`, `unsupported`, `unknown`.
- No source-file or changed-line quotas. A shared helper or small new module is allowed when
  source evidence supports it. Not allowed: unrelated refactoring, guessed generalization,
  broad admission of formerly unsupported behaviour, conflicting edits to another writer's files.
- A blocker inside the open family's source-proved dependency chain is completed within the
  family. A blocker belonging to another family closes/checkpoints the current scope, records
  the boundary, and becomes the next family assignment - never a second worker redoing finished
  work.

## Timeline changes: adjudicate on source evidence, never on frame targets

- Keep the frame-5913 correction; keep historical receipts with an explicit note of why the old
  source-authentic claim was withdrawn.
- Compare candidate and baseline on **equivalent input at the first divergent callback**, then
  adjudicate against the pinned source evidence: reject what disagrees with the source, allow
  and document what exposes an old baseline error.
- Never optimize for recreating an old world hash, for a monotonically increasing frontier, or
  for passing a stale acceptance report.
- Keep the **5940 unsupported-domain negative fixture** until that behaviour is independently
  source-proved and intentionally expanded. It is not a permanent frame ceiling; passing it on
  another authentic trajectory does not admit the fixture.

## Positive comparisons must prove successful execution

1. Rust execution success, `result.status == passed`, the intended callback/caller, frame/slot,
   input/build/source/resource identity, no callback error, input roundtrip.
2. Completed independent instruction-reference execution (not merely `source_prepared`), with
   environmental assumptions and deferred ports documented.
3. **Exact lengths of all eight buffers before any equality check** (pinned layout: backing
   343,958 bytes, total 427,369), then zero differences. Never `zip`-compare as an extent check.
4. Expected RNG state, draw count and relevant **draw order**, plus journal/side-effect requests,
   record writes and callback outcome - not only identical final bytes.
5. Positive and negative cases reported **separately**; a negative gate proves the expected
   rejection/error AND complete transactional world/RNG preservation. Rollback-produced byte
   equality is not a successful callback. Never adjust expected values to green a gate.

After coherent changes: affected-caller comparisons + focused regressions. At each family
integration milestone: Rust suites, release build, **ordinary campaign replay on the corrected
timeline**, and **native input/framebuffer/visual checks on the same frozen executable** before
claiming integrated acceptance. One heavyweight job at a time; `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`.

## Initial family (consolidation)

**Mule light/full callback sequencing, shared completed-animation and hit-reaction continuation,
and affected owned/neutral callers.** Movement/cell-commit and linked-AI dependencies only where
source evidence connects them. Matrix: `mule-family-caller-domain-matrix.json`. Do not widen to
all combat, genetics, strategic AI or every creature type; do not commission parallel repairs or
rewrite completed changes; preserve original owner-specific semantics and unsupported
blood/lethal/operator/alternate-query branches unless separately proved.

Closure = declared caller/transition domains and impacted shared paths have evidence-backed
successful execution, integrated ordinary/native acceptance where applicable, and any new
out-of-scope blocker is clearly identified. A frame number is not closure.

## Orchestration and cost

Keep the DeepSeek director / pinned GLM-5.3 Flash worker routing, root ownership, standing
approval for ordinary paid calls, resource protections and evidence immutability. No new
provider, arbitrary dollar cap, parallel A/B implementation, extra orchestration layer, or
original-DOS debugging experiment. The original-game observation gap remains a separate
conditional research task; the bounded interpreter is not the retail game running live. After
two non-productive attempts in a family, change the investigation, not the patch wording.
