**Blocked on exact command evidence, not on the emulator.** A direct targeted read of CONQ001 finds forty active plants and one active numeric-family0 record, all with owner byte4. Its serialized family2 player-actor group is empty. This loaded-state subset has no proved controllable actor for an admitted player ID0..3. Numeric owner4 is retained as data; this review does not invent an ownership-transfer rule.

The manual describes Botanist left-click on a plant to harvest seeds and Control+left-click to cut it (control/specialist sections, PDF7–9 / printed10–14). These are narrow candidate commands with existing loaded plant targets. Their native modifier binding, role/category mapping, target eligibility, actor task setter and resulting writes remain unknown. The category0/2 creation requests from mask5 do not alone establish a live, initialized Botanist. Camera movement, selection/clear, automatic reveal and a debug retirement button do not satisfy a source-backed player mutation command.

A partial existing controller export supplies a useful eligibility witness. At2CD44..2CEAC it requires selected entity owner+0x18 to equal the selected player's record byte+1; it then accepts active type1 or3, excluding type2 plants as command recipients. The type3 branch excludes signed state+0x55 values2/3/4/5 and requires byte+0x70 bit0. It emits packet byte+0xA value3 with coordinates/ID; the type1 branch emits0x11. These values remain opaque until the actual click route and command consumer are pinned. Newly placed actor flags word+0x70=`0x0400` leaves the required low bit clear, so a preceding activation/task transition matters. The export begins mid-function; its local predicates are not a complete global input contract.

The Rust plant world can retain the loaded forty records, links, cell counters and totals and advance only independently proved plant branches. It must explicitly suspend other numeric families and report pending target/UI/audio/bookkeeping cleanup instead of declaring a full original tick. Even a correctly applied retirement transaction does not prove autonomous effects from earlier family0/player callbacks were preserved. Random/spawn/later-counter branches must be implemented from evidence or stop explicitly; invented cadence, growth rates or successful work completion would change the game.

The next narrow deliverable is a command-submission transaction: validate one genuinely controllable actor and an existing plant target, apply the exact task/target/state writes, and distinguish acceptance from later travel/work/completion.
