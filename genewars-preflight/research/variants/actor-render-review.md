# Actor indexed8 compositor review

Corroborated against the eight pinned CD-candidate listings in `actor-draw-contract.json`, with independent native output comparisons. This is a component color-pass review, not an integrated world draw or original execution result.

The independent byte-offset/RLE oracle checked all 16 exact staged actor resources: 1,552 sprite records including every palette index, opacity bit and consumed-byte count; 1,982 animation-relative frames and 2,108 component references. Four placements/flag settings per frame produced 7,928 complete frame comparisons. A further 20,480 synthetic draws cover all low global/component flag combinations, four owners, literal zero, owner-color endpoints, overlapping components, fade/blend order, clipping and full-range i32 origins. Eight rejection cases preserve the entire destination. The wrapper comparison covers 65,536 explicit class/mode/actor-field cases. Total compared framebuffer bytes: 44,626,648. Exact source/input/output hashes and counts are in `actor-render-review.json`; replay with `python -B research/variants/actor-render-review.py`.

One concrete issue was found and corrected by the owning lane: adding signed component offsets to public i32 screen origins could overflow. The compositor now widens placement and clipping arithmetic to i64; extreme origins clip without changing destination bytes. The widened behavior is an explicit safe API boundary, rather than a claim of source wrapping behavior outside its ordinary screen-coordinate domain.

Direct source checks support raw shade +17 selecting the fade branch, virtual height selecting its row, owner recolor before fade, and the two distinct blend-table operand orders. Whole-frame staging protects earlier component writes when a later component rejects. Normal world shadow, labels, actual fade/blend table acquisition, projection and scene order remain caller responsibilities. High component flags remain rejected. Complete final-EOL decoding is stricter validation than the source's possible final-row early return. The all-resource native comparison passed after the overflow correction; no additional actionable compositor mismatch was found within this boundary.
