# Owned actor initialization boundary

The existing `plan_player_actor_creation` helper can already be used as a bounded selection/allocation plan. Its output is intentionally a request, not a complete initialized actor. The following inputs and mutations distinguish the loaded file from an owned runtime actor; the emulator is not a dependency.

| Stage | Source-backed facts | Remaining caller obligation |

4FD30 computes intermediate height+0x11 and shade+0x17 from position global112D10 before80830 overwrites position. Its mapped initial dword is zero; the targeted direct-reference graph finds two reads and no direct writes. That does not exclude indirect mutation. 56E20 does not recalculate height after placement. Later72960 refreshes shade for active family2 records, but this does not license replacing stored height with target height. Preserve the intermediate-origin input/height or explicitly gate a known initial-zero origin rather than derive a convenient value from the final target.

Two distinctions matter for Milestone C. Category0/2 are machine labels here until a resource/UI witness maps them to specialist roles. Also, freshly placed flags70word0x0400 keeps the producer's required low active bit clear. A legitimate control-activation transition must be traced before claiming the new actor accepts movement/work commands. The smallest honest progression is typed blueprint initialization, exact placement/resource handles, then a source-backed actor command-submission transaction. Travel, harvest completion, AI and other-family ticks can remain separately suspended.
