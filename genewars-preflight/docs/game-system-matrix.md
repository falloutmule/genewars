# GeneWars game-system matrix (provisional)

This matrix records the supplied DOS manual as documentation evidence. Page citations use **PDF page / printed page**; for a two-page spread, printed numbers are listed left-to-right. The PDF is a scan, so OCR at `private/manual/extracted-text.txt` is only a private working aid. Source SHA-256: `0de3be7b3ba15d68af16744f4b742d549b840cf33162581aeb7de509bdf29309`.

Evidence labels: **manual-documented** means the manual states or shows the behavior; **binary-verified** means statically confirmed in a named executable; **execution-verified** means observed while running one; **disputed** means sources conflict; **unknown** means evidence does not establish the rule. This lane verified no binary or execution behavior, so all positive rules below remain manual-documented. The manual's notice says the product may change without notice.

| System | Evidence and documented behavior | Status | Evidence gap / next verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Controls | Left-click selects/moves/issues contextual actions; right-click deselects; Control+drag groups specialists; Alt+drag selects creatures only in mixed areas. Engineer build/repair/upgrade, Botanist planting/harvesting/cutting, Geneticist research/heal/stun, and Ranger feed/fight/mate/stun/kill/cull actions are described. Network chat uses F1-F4 and Return. (PDF 7-9, 15 / printed 10-14, 26) | manual-documented | Executable event names, targeting state, input edge behavior, and exact command semantics are unknown. |

Strength/health and creature evolution or Botanist seed count are displayed. (PDF 7 / printed 10-11) | manual-documented | Layout coordinates, numeric ranges, refresh cadence and tooltip logic are unknown. |
| Specialists | Engineer builds and maintains; Geneticist studies/heals/stuns; Botanist farms and identifies plants; Ranger manages creature behavior and culls; pilot handles planet travel. Later crew choice exposes names and expertise. (PDF 6-10 / printed 8-17) | manual-documented | Skill formulas, rates, selection rules and crew persistence are unknown. |
| Buildings | Extractor converts minerals to GOOP and must come first; Distributor carries power and can support itself plus one Extractor; Photon Processor generates power; Gene Pod makes basic drones; Recycler turns dead biomass into GOOP; Vat stores GOOP; Landing Pad transfers crew; Pulper makes upgrade mouldings. (PDF 9-11 / printed 15-18) | manual-documented | Costs, rates, ranges, capacities, power consumption and unlock flags are unknown. More buildings are introduced later but not enumerated here. |
| Economy and upgrades | GOOP is the building/creature material. A building upgrade uses Engineer + powered Pulper + at least one Mule + one Crab; Mule brings plant material, Crab carries mouldings and assists the Engineer. Higher Engineer skill speeds the upgrade. (PDF 5, 10-11 / printed 6-7, 17-18) | manual-documented | Transaction order, costs, work rates, failure behavior and exact upgrade effects are unknown. |
| Species and genetics | Manual names Mule, Crab, Frog, Bird and Dino; 50% research is said to permit Gene Pod creation, with a 50% ceiling there. More research affects size, strength, stamina and intelligence; Gene Machine/Lab are named for further research and hybrid creation. Monoliths can improve specialists and grant species powers. (PDF 6, 8, 10-12 / printed 8-9, 13, 16, 18-20) | manual-documented | Genetic/stat formulas, thresholds beyond stated prose, build availability and Gene Machine/Lab progression are unknown. |
| Breeding and hybrids | Ranger presence is required for mating; season is signaled by a red heart. Different parents may produce a hybrid with abilities of both, treated as a new species; powers do not automatically transfer. (PDF 9, 12 / printed 14, 20) | manual-documented | Compatibility, probability, timing, offspring stats and species discovery are unknown. |
| Flora and terrain | Plant species have surface preferences and differing food, wood, GOOP or defensive value. Botanist seed harvest reveals plant information; interface symbols indicate food, wood and seed requirements. Early plants include Terrinium Bulb, Phylax Root and Waxx Leaf. (PDF 12-13 / printed 20-22) | manual-documented | Complete species list, growth cycle, terrain codes and numeric values are unknown. |
| Mission/campaign | Single-player is described as 24 planets. Initial hint missions: Exceland, Krell, Madami, Roach and Brown; mission tools/objectives differ. Ethereals provide orders and assess player conduct. (PDF 6, 13-14 / printed 9, 22-23) | manual-documented | Exact objective predicates, mission scripts/order, timers and transition behavior are unknown. |
| Ethereals and scoring | Bad-o-meter reflects approval; good performance may bring rewards such as Monoliths, while bad conduct may be punished. Network victory uses Zone score when Zones exist, otherwise defeating opponents. (PDF 5, 7, 15 / printed 6-7, 10, 26) | manual-documented | No numeric approval thresholds or single-player win/loss formula are documented. |
| Network mode | Manual describes IPX discovery, sessions, host-selected level, eight levels, opponent chat using F1-F4, team colors, and a creature advantage choice (Dino/Crab/Frog/Bird at 50% research with power active). (PDF 14-15 / printed 24-26) | manual-documented | Actual networking protocol, player cap, synchronization and lobby state are unknown. |
| Options, saves and travel | Options diagram labels music toggle and level, sound-effects toggle and level, save-new-file, load-saved-game, return to main screen, exit-to-system and exit-screen. Pilot handles interplanetary travel; Landing Pad transfers crew. (PDF 6, 10, 13 / printed 9, 17, 22) | manual-documented | Save schema, write timing, campaign persistence and travel state are unknown. |
| AI/autonomy | Manual mentions computer colonization opponents and says deactivated creatures follow their own instincts. It provides no opponent AI or autonomous behavior algorithm. (PDF 7, 13 / printed 10, 23) | unknown | Needs executable tracing and controlled observation against a hash-identified build. |

No within-manual rule conflict was identified in the sections reviewed. The table deliberately omits any inferred values or algorithms. All claims are **manual-documented only**; **binary-verified: none; execution-verified: none**. No contradictory finding is currently recorded.
